# Week 3 — Workout B — Hip Thrust Heavy + Vertical Press + Core
**Phase**: 1 — Foundation | **Week**: 3 of 12 | **Rounds**: 3 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Glutes · Shoulders · Back · Core (anti-extension)

> Rest 60–90 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Elliptical `3 min`

### 2. Clamshells `15 each side`

### 3. Scapular Wall Slide `10 reps`

### 4. Hip Hinge with Pause `10 reps`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Glute Dominant + Shoulder (bench/DB area) (3 rounds)

### 5. Barbell Hip Thrust `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Glutes, hamstrings, core
- **Clint**: bar + 45s | **Wife**: bar only or light plates
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 60–90 sec after each round.

### 6. Dumbbell Shoulder Press (seated) `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Deltoids, triceps, core
- **Clint**: 35 lb DBs | **Wife**: 15 lb DBs

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Pull + Step (cable/step area) (3 rounds)

### 7. Assisted Pull-Up or Cable Pulldown (neutral grip) `3 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, biceps, rear delts
- **Clint**: bodyweight | **Wife**: assisted or 50 lb cable
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 60–90 sec after each round.

### 8. Step-Up with Knee Drive (DB) `3 × 10 each leg`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, hip flexors
- **Clint**: 25 lb DBs | **Wife**: 12 lb DBs

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (3 rounds)

### 9. Cable Pallof Press (kneeling) `3 × 12 each side`

### 10. V-Up `3 × 10`

### 11. Dead Bug with Dumbbell (arm only) `3 × 10 each side`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Rowing Machine `4 × 30 sec hard / 30 sec easy`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Barbell hip thrust: most effective glute builder — maximize squeeze at top.
- Step-up with knee drive: adds balance and functional hip flexor challenge.
