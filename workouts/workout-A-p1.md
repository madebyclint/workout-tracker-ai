# Workout A — Push + Core + Conditioning
**Phase**: 1 — Foundation | **Weeks**: 1–4 | **Est. Time**: 35–40 min
**Focus**: Chest · Shoulders · Quads · Glutes · Core (anti-extension)

> Rest 60–90 sec between supersets. 90 sec between rounds. Form over load.
> Set up both stations in a circuit before starting the round.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Cardio Warm-Up `2 min brisk walk or bike`
- Elevate heart rate. Loosen hips before loading.

### 2. World's Greatest Stretch `5 each side`
- Deep lunge + thoracic rotation. Slow and deliberate each rep.

### 3. Band Pull-Aparts `15 reps`
- Light band at chest height. Retract shoulder blades fully.

### 4. Glute Bridge `15 reps`
- Bodyweight. Drive through heels. Activate glutes before squatting.

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1: Lower Body (rack or floor area)

### 5. Goblet Squat `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, core
- **Clint**: 45–55 lb KB | **Wife**: 20–30 lb KB
- Feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out. Deep squat, chest tall. Superset with Hip Thrust.

### 6. Hip Thrust (Glute Bridge) `3 × 15`
- **Target muscles**: Glutes, hamstrings, core
- **Clint**: bodyweight or 45 lb bar | **Wife**: bodyweight
- Upper back on bench. Drive through heels. 2-sec squeeze at top.
- Rest 60–90 sec after completing both exercises each round.

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2: Push Upper Body (cable or bench area)

### 7. Dumbbell Bench Press `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Chest, front delts, triceps
- **Clint**: 60–70 lbs total (2 DBs) | **Wife**: 20–30 lbs total
- Feet flat, neutral spine. 3-second eccentric. Chest focus — moderate load.

### 8. Dumbbell Lateral Raise `3 × 15`
- **Target muscles**: Medial deltoids, shoulder health
- **Clint**: 15–20 lbs each | **Wife**: 5–8 lbs each
- Light. Lead with elbows, not wrists. No traps. Superset with Bench Press.
- Rest 60 sec after completing both exercises each round.

---

## Block 4 — Core Circuit (mat or cable area)

### 9. Dead Bug `3 × 10 each side`
- Lower back pressed flat throughout. Slow and controlled each rep.

### 10. Pallof Press `3 × 12 each side`
- **Target muscles**: Core (anti-rotation), obliques
- **Clint**: 30–40 lbs | **Wife**: 10–15 lbs
- Stand sideways to cable. Slow press out. Resist rotation the whole time.

### 11. Plank to Down Dog `3 × 10`
- Start in plank, push hips up to down dog. Hold 1 sec each position.
- Minimal rest between core exercises.

---

## Block 5 — Finisher (5 min)

### 12. Jump Rope or Rowing Machine `3 × 45 sec on / 15 sec off`
- Max effort. Keep intensity high. Short rest is the point.

---

## Notes
- No direct arm isolation — bench and rows hit arms sufficiently without adding size.
- Lateral raises stay light intentionally — shoulder health and posture, not mass.
- Phase 1 priority: learn the movement patterns. Load increases in Phase 2.
- On busy gym days: swap circuits to straight sets — keep the same exercises.
- Eat protein within 1 hour post-workout.
