# Week 7 — Workout A — Paused Squat + Wide Pull + Advanced Core
**Phase**: 2 — Build | **Week**: 7 of 12 | **Rounds**: 3 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Quads · Lats · Glutes · Core (anti-rotation)

> Rest 45–60 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Treadmill (incline 5, 3.5 mph) `3 min`

### 2. Glute Bridge `15 reps`

### 3. Deep Squat Hold `30 sec`

### 4. Arm Circles + Band Pull `15 reps`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Paused Squat + Lat Pull (rack/cable area) (3 rounds)

### 5. Goblet Squat with 2-sec Pause at Bottom `3 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, core, mobility
- **Clint**: 50 lb KB | **Wife**: 30 lb KB
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 45–60 sec after each round.

### 6. Lat Pulldown (Underhand Grip) `3 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, biceps, rear delts
- **Clint**: 130 lb | **Wife**: 60 lb

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Hip Thrust Variation + Press (bench area) (3 rounds)

### 7. Single-Leg Hip Thrust (bench) `3 × 12 each leg`
- **Target muscles**: Glutes (unilateral), hamstrings
- **Clint**: 35 lb DB on hip | **Wife**: bodyweight
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 45–60 sec after each round.

### 8. Dumbbell Chest Fly (flat bench) `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Chest, front delts
- **Clint**: 35 lb DBs | **Wife**: 15 lb DBs

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (3 rounds)

### 9. Tall Kneeling Pallof Press `3 × 15 each side`

### 10. V-Up with Hold `3 × 10`

### 11. RKC Plank `3 × 20 sec`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Stationary Bike `6 × 20 sec sprint / 40 sec easy`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Paused squat eliminates stretch reflex — builds raw strength and control.
- Underhand lat pulldown shifts emphasis toward lower lats and biceps.
