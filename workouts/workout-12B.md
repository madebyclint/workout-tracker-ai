# Week 12 — Workout B — Peak Lower Dominant + Full Core Circuit
**Phase**: 3 — Peak | **Week**: 12 of 12 | **Rounds**: 4 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Glutes · Hamstrings · Quads · Core (peak all planes)

> Rest 30–45 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Treadmill (incline 6, 3.8 mph) `3 min`

### 2. Glute Bridge `20 reps`

### 3. Hip Flexor Stretch `45 sec each side`

### 4. Band Lateral Walk `20 each side`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Glute + Hinge Heavy (bench/barbell area) (4 rounds)

### 5. Barbell Hip Thrust (peak load) `4 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Glutes, hamstrings, core
- **Clint**: bar + 45 + 45 lbs | **Wife**: bar + 35s
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 30–45 sec after each round.

### 6. Romanian Deadlift (peak load) `4 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Hamstrings, glutes, lower back
- **Clint**: 75 lb DBs | **Wife**: 35 lb DBs

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Single Leg + Row (DB/cable area) (4 rounds)

### 7. Bulgarian Split Squat `4 × 6 each leg`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, hip flexors
- **Clint**: 45 lb DBs | **Wife**: 25 lb DBs
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 30–45 sec after each round.

### 8. Half-Kneeling Single-Arm Cable Row `4 × 10 each side`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, rhomboids, core stability
- **Clint**: 65 lb | **Wife**: 32 lb

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (4 rounds)

### 9. Ab Wheel Rollout `3 × 10`

### 10. Copenhagen Plank `3 × 30 sec each side`

### 11. Dragon Flag Negative `3 × 6`

### 12. Cable Pallof Press `3 × 15 each side`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 13. Rowing Machine `5 × 1 min hard / 20 sec rest`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Peak session — maximum loads. Record everything.
