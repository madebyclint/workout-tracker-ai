# Week 3 — Workout A — Deadlift Focus + Incline Push + Core
**Phase**: 1 — Foundation | **Week**: 3 of 12 | **Rounds**: 3 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Hamstrings · Glutes · Upper Chest · Upper Back · Core (stability)

> Rest 60–90 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Treadmill `3 min moderate`

### 2. Good Morning (bodyweight) `10 reps`

### 3. Inchworm `5 reps`

### 4. Band Glute Bridge `15 reps`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Hinge Heavy + Push (barbell/DB area) (3 rounds)

### 5. Dumbbell Deadlift `3 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Hamstrings, glutes, lower back
- **Clint**: 60–70 lb DBs | **Wife**: 25–30 lb DBs
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 60–90 sec after each round.

### 6. Incline Dumbbell Press `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Upper chest, front delts, triceps
- **Clint**: 40 lb DBs | **Wife**: 20 lb DBs

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Upper Back + Lunge (same free weight area) (3 rounds)

### 7. Dumbbell Walking Lunge `3 × 10 each leg`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, hip flexors
- **Clint**: 25 lb DBs | **Wife**: 15 lb DBs
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 60–90 sec after each round.

### 8. Dumbbell Bent-Over Row `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, rhomboids, rear delts, biceps
- **Clint**: 45 lb DBs | **Wife**: 20 lb DBs

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (3 rounds)

### 9. Stir the Pot (forearm plank, circles) `3 × 8 each direction`

### 10. Hanging Knee Raise `3 × 12`

### 11. Pallof Press Hold `3 × 20 sec each side`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Stationary Bike `5 × 20 sec all-out / 40 sec easy`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Stir the pot: forearms on stability ball or plate, draw circles. Extremely effective stabilizer.
- Incline press targets upper chest that flat bench misses.
