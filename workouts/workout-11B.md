# Week 11 — Workout B — Deadlift Max + Complex Upper + Core
**Phase**: 3 — Peak | **Week**: 11 of 12 | **Rounds**: 4 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Full Posterior Chain · Chest · Quads · Core (advanced)

> Rest 30–45 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Bike (hard) `3 min`

### 2. Hip Hinge Drill `10 reps`

### 3. Scapular Activation `10 reps`

### 4. Glute Bridge `20 reps`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Heavy Deadlift + Pendlay Row (barbell area) (4 rounds)

### 5. Conventional Deadlift (near max) `4 × 5`
- **Target muscles**: Full posterior chain, core
- **Clint**: 175–195 lb | **Wife**: 90–100 lb
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 30–45 sec after each round.

### 6. Pendlay Row (explosive) `4 × 6`
- **Target muscles**: Back, lats, rhomboids (power emphasis)
- **Clint**: 95 lb | **Wife**: 45 lb

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Split Squat + Chest (bench/DB area) (4 rounds)

### 7. Bulgarian Split Squat (heaviest yet) `4 × 6 each leg`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, hip flexors
- **Clint**: 45 lb DBs | **Wife**: 25 lb DBs
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 30–45 sec after each round.

### 8. Dumbbell Decline Press `4 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Lower chest, triceps, front delts
- **Clint**: 50 lb DBs | **Wife**: 25 lb DBs

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (4 rounds)

### 9. Ab Wheel Full Rollout `4 × 8`

### 10. Stir the Pot (slow) `4 × 10 each direction`

### 11. Pallof Press Lunge `4 × 8 each side`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Jump Rope `5 × 1 min / 20 sec rest`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Near-max deadlift: prioritize form. Record your load — compare to Week 1 and Week 8.
- Pendlay row: explosive from the floor, builds raw back power.
