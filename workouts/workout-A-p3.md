# Workout A — Push + Core + Conditioning
**Phase**: 3 — Peak | **Weeks**: 9–12 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Chest · Shoulders · Quads · Glutes · Core (anti-extension)

> Heaviest loads of the program. Rest 30–45 sec between supersets (metabolic stress).
> 4 rounds on Circuit 1 and finisher. Maximum effort — this is the peak.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Cardio Warm-Up `2 min brisk walk or bike`
- Elevate heart rate. Loosen hips before loading.

### 2. World's Greatest Stretch `5 each side`
- Deep lunge + thoracic rotation.

### 3. Band Pull-Aparts `15 reps`
- Retract shoulder blades fully.

### 4. Glute Bridge `15 reps`
- Bodyweight. Activate glutes before squatting.

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1: Lower Body (rack or floor area)

### 5. Goblet Squat `4 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, core
- **Clint**: 65–75 lb KB | **Wife**: 30–40 lb KB
- Heaviest loads. 4 rounds. Slow and controlled. Superset with Hip Thrust.

### 6. Hip Thrust (Glute Bridge) `4 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Glutes, hamstrings, core
- **Clint**: 95–115 lb bar | **Wife**: 45–55 lbs
- Heaviest of the program. Drive hard through heels.
- Rest 30–45 sec after completing both exercises each round.

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2: Push Upper Body (cable or bench area)

### 7. Dumbbell Bench Press `4 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Chest, front delts, triceps
- **Clint**: 80–95 lbs total (2 DBs) | **Wife**: 30–40 lbs total
- Heaviest loads. 4 rounds. Controlled descent essential.

### 8. Dumbbell Lateral Raise `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Medial deltoids, shoulder health
- **Clint**: 20–25 lbs each | **Wife**: 8–12 lbs each
- Controlled. Don't chase weight on lateral raises — shoulder health over mass.
- Rest 30–45 sec after completing both exercises each round.

---

## Block 4 — Core Circuit (mat or cable area)

### 9. Dead Bug `3 × 12 slow tempo`
- 3-second lowering phase each rep. Maximum control and tension.

### 10. Pallof Press `3 × 15 each side`
- **Target muscles**: Core (anti-rotation), obliques
- **Clint**: 45–55 lbs | **Wife**: 20–25 lbs
- Heaviest loads. Pause 2 sec at full extension.

### 11. Plank to Down Dog `3 × 15`
- Full range. No momentum. Each transition controlled.
- Minimal rest between core exercises.

---

## Block 5 — Finisher (5 min)

### 12. Jump Rope or Rowing Machine `4 × 45 sec on / 15 sec off`
- 4 rounds (vs 3 in earlier phases). Max intensity every interval.

---

## Notes
- Peak phase: heaviest loads, shortest rest, highest metabolic demand of the program.
- The 4th round on Circuit 1 and finisher is the key Phase 3 upgrade.
- After finishing Phase 3, restart Phase 1 with heavier starting loads — you've set a new baseline.
- Shoulder health: lateral raises intentionally stay light even in Phase 3.
