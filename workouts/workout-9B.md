# Week 9 — Workout B — Trap Bar + Landmine Row + Rotational Core
**Phase**: 3 — Peak | **Week**: 9 of 12 | **Rounds**: 4 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Full Posterior Chain · Back · Quads · Core (rotation)

> Rest 30–45 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Rowing Machine (moderate-hard) `3 min`

### 2. Hip Hinge Drill `10 reps`

### 3. Scapular Activation `10 reps`

### 4. Hip Flexor Stretch `30 sec each side`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Trap Bar + Landmine Row (barbell area) (4 rounds)

### 5. Trap Bar Deadlift (heavy) `4 × 6`
- **Target muscles**: Full posterior chain, quads, core
- **Clint**: 155 lb | **Wife**: 80 lb
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 30–45 sec after each round.

### 6. Landmine Row `4 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, rhomboids, rear delts
- **Clint**: bar + 35 lb | **Wife**: bar + 15 lb

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Split Squat + Press (bench/DB area) (4 rounds)

### 7. Front Foot Elevated Split Squat `4 × 8 each leg`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, hip flexors
- **Clint**: 40 lb DBs | **Wife**: 20 lb DBs
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 30–45 sec after each round.

### 8. Standing DB Shoulder Press `4 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Deltoids, triceps, core
- **Clint**: 35 lb DBs | **Wife**: 15 lb DBs

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (4 rounds)

### 9. Cable Woodchop (standing, fast) `4 × 12 each side`

### 10. Hanging Leg Raise with Twist `4 × 10`

### 11. Hollow Body Hold `4 × 30 sec`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Jump Rope `5 × 1 min / 30 sec rest`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Front foot elevated split squat: maximum quad depth — most demanding split squat variation.
- Landmine row: joint-friendly and allows heavy loading.
