# Week 11 — Workout A — Squat Variation + Advanced Pull + Core Peak
**Phase**: 3 — Peak | **Week**: 11 of 12 | **Rounds**: 4 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Quads · Lats · Hamstrings · Shoulders · Core (advanced all planes)

> Rest 30–45 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Rowing Machine (hard) `3 min`

### 2. Goblet Squat (slow) `10 reps`

### 3. Hip Flexor Stretch `45 sec each side`

### 4. Band Pull-Aparts `25 reps`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Paused Squat Heavy + Weighted Pull (rack/cable) (4 rounds)

### 5. Barbell Squat with 2-sec Pause `4 × 6`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, core, raw strength
- **Clint**: bar + 45s each side | **Wife**: 55 lb goblet
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 30–45 sec after each round.

### 6. Weighted Lat Pulldown with Hold `4 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, biceps, rear delts
- **Clint**: 145 lb | **Wife**: 70 lb

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Hip Hinge + Press Complex (same zone) (4 rounds)

### 7. Single-Leg Deadlift (KB) `4 × 8 each leg`
- **Target muscles**: Hamstrings, glutes, balance
- **Clint**: 44 lb KB | **Wife**: 26 lb KB
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 30–45 sec after each round.

### 8. Push Press (DB) `4 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Deltoids, triceps, power, core
- **Clint**: 40 lb DBs | **Wife**: 18 lb DBs

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (4 rounds)

### 9. Hanging Windshield Wiper `4 × 6 each side`

### 10. Kneeling Cable Crunch with Rotation `4 × 12 each side`

### 11. RKC Plank `4 × 25 sec`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Rowing Machine `5 × 1 min hard / 20 sec rest`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Paused squat: removes bounce, demands full strength from bottom — elite strength builder.
- Hanging windshield wiper: requires significant lat and hip flexor strength.
