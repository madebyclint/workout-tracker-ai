# Week 10 — Workout B — KB Complex + Vertical Push + Core
**Phase**: 3 — Peak | **Week**: 10 of 12 | **Rounds**: 4 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Full Body Power · Shoulders · Lats · Core (advanced flexion)

> Rest 30–45 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Treadmill `3 min`

### 2. KB Halo `10 each direction`

### 3. Hip Circles `10 each side`

### 4. Scapular Push-Up `10 reps`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — KB Complex + Press (kettlebell/DB area) (4 rounds)

### 5. KB Swing to Goblet Squat (combo) `4 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Glutes, hamstrings, quads, core (power combo)
- **Clint**: 35 lb KB | **Wife**: 18 lb KB
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 30–45 sec after each round.

### 6. Dumbbell Z-Press (seated on floor) `4 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Deltoids, triceps, core (no back support)
- **Clint**: 30 lb DBs | **Wife**: 12 lb DBs

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Pulldown + Step (cable/bench area) (4 rounds)

### 7. Lat Pulldown (wide, 3-sec eccentric) `4 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, biceps, rear delts
- **Clint**: 140 lb | **Wife**: 65 lb
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 30–45 sec after each round.

### 8. Weighted Step-Up to Balance `4 × 10 each leg`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, balance, hip stability
- **Clint**: 35 lb DBs | **Wife**: 18 lb DBs

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (4 rounds)

### 9. Dragon Flag Negative (slow lower) `4 × 6`

### 10. Side Plank with Hip Abduction `4 × 12 each side`

### 11. Slow Leg Raise with Hip Pop `4 × 10`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Stationary Bike `6 × 25 sec all-out / 35 sec rest`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Z-Press eliminates all back support — pure shoulder and core demand.
- KB swing to goblet combo is highly metabolic — maintain form under fatigue.
