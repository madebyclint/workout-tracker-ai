# Workout B — Pull + Hinge + Core + Conditioning
**Phase**: 3 — Peak | **Weeks**: 9–12 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Back · Hamstrings · Glutes · Rear Delts · Core (anti-lateral/rotation)

> Heaviest loads of the program. Rest 30–45 sec between supersets (metabolic stress).
> 4 rounds on Circuit 1 and finisher. Maximum effort — this is the peak.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Cardio Warm-Up `2 min row or elliptical`
- Moderate pace. Open up thoracic spine.

### 2. Cat-Cow `10 reps`
- Full spinal flexion and extension. Slow.

### 3. Hip Hinge Drill `10 reps`
- Groove the pattern before loading.

### 4. Face Pull with Band `15 reps`
- External rotation at top. Warms up rear delts.

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1: Hinge + Pull (cable or free weight area)

### 5. Romanian Deadlift (RDL) `4 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Hamstrings, glutes, lower back
- **Clint**: 135–155 lbs (barbell) | **Wife**: 60–70 lbs (DBs)
- Heaviest loads. 4 rounds. Perfect hinge — form does not break.

### 6. Single-Arm Dumbbell Row `4 × 8 each side`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, rhomboids, rear delts, biceps
- **Clint**: 65–75 lb DB | **Wife**: 30–35 lb DB
- Heaviest loads. 4 rounds. Full stretch and full contraction.
- Superset with RDL. Rest 30–45 sec after completing both exercises each round.

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2: Vertical Pull + Rear Delt (cable machine)

### 7. Lat Pulldown (Wide Grip) `4 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, biceps, rear delts
- **Clint**: 130–145 lbs | **Wife**: 70–80 lbs
- Heaviest loads. 4 rounds. No swinging.

### 8. Cable Face Pull `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Rear delts, external rotators, rhomboids
- **Clint**: 55–65 lbs | **Wife**: 25–30 lbs
- Heavier than Phase 2. Form is everything on face pulls — never sacrifice it.
- Superset with Lat Pulldown. Rest 30–45 sec after completing both exercises each round.

---

## Block 4 — Core Circuit (mat area)

### 9. Side Plank with Hip Dip `3 × 15 each side`
- Most reps of the program. Hips don't rotate.

### 10. Hollow Body Hold `3 × 40 sec`
- 10 sec longer than Phase 2. Lower back stays flat entire hold.

### 11. Cable Woodchop (Low to High) `3 × 12 each side`
- **Target muscles**: Obliques, core (anti-rotation), shoulder
- **Clint**: 45–55 lbs | **Wife**: 20–25 lbs
- Heaviest loads. Drive through hips. Control the return.
- Minimal rest between core exercises.

---

## Block 5 — Finisher (5 min)

### 12. Sled Push or Kettlebell Swing `4 × 40 sec on / 20 sec off`
- 4 rounds (vs 3 in earlier phases). Max power output every interval.

---

## Notes
- Peak phase: the 4th round on Circuit 1 and the finisher is the key Phase 3 upgrade.
- RDL at this load is exceptional for hiking, bouldering, and general posterior chain strength.
- Hollow body 40-sec holds are elite — wife will likely excel here given dancer background.
- After completing Phase 3, restart Phase 1 with heavier starting loads — a new baseline.
