# Week 4 — Workout A — Front Squat + Single-Arm Pull + Core
**Phase**: 1 — Foundation | **Week**: 4 of 12 | **Rounds**: 3 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Quads · Back · Glutes · Core (flexion)

> Rest 60–90 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Bike `3 min`

### 2. Ankle Mobility Drill `10 each side`

### 3. Thoracic Extension over Foam Roller `60 sec`

### 4. Glute Bridge `15 reps`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Quad + Pull (rack/cable area) (3 rounds)

### 5. Dumbbell Front Squat `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, core, front delts
- **Clint**: 40 lb DBs | **Wife**: 20 lb DBs
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 60–90 sec after each round.

### 6. Single-Arm Cable Row `3 × 12 each side`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, rhomboids, rear delts, biceps
- **Clint**: 60 lb | **Wife**: 30 lb

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Posterior Chain + Push (same area) (3 rounds)

### 7. Good Morning (bar or DB) `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Hamstrings, glutes, lower back
- **Clint**: 45 lb bar | **Wife**: 20 lb DBs
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 60–90 sec after each round.

### 8. Cable Fly (chest) `3 × 15`
- **Target muscles**: Chest, front delts
- **Clint**: 30 lb each side | **Wife**: 15 lb each side

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (3 rounds)

### 9. Lying Leg Raise `3 × 12`

### 10. Side Plank with Reach Through `3 × 10 each side`

### 11. Bird Dog `3 × 10 each side`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Jump Rope or Fast Stepping `4 × 40 sec / 20 sec rest`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Front squat demands more core and upper back than back squat — keep elbows high.
- Good mornings: excellent hamstring and lower back strength builder.
