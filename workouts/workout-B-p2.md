# Workout B — Pull + Hinge + Core + Conditioning
**Phase**: 2 — Build | **Weeks**: 5–8 | **Est. Time**: 35–40 min
**Focus**: Back · Hamstrings · Glutes · Rear Delts · Core (anti-lateral/rotation)

> Add 5–10% load vs Phase 1 to all major lifts. Rest 45–60 sec between supersets.
> Rule: if you hit top of rep range with good form all 3 sets → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Cardio Warm-Up `2 min row or elliptical`
- Moderate pace. Open up thoracic spine.

### 2. Cat-Cow `10 reps`
- Full spinal flexion and extension. Slow.

### 3. Hip Hinge Drill `10 reps`
- Groove the pattern before loading.

### 4. Face Pull with Band `15 reps`
- External rotation at top. Warms up rear delts.

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1: Hinge + Pull (cable or free weight area)

### 5. Romanian Deadlift (RDL) `3 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Hamstrings, glutes, lower back
- **Clint**: 115–135 lbs (barbell) | **Wife**: 50–60 lbs (DBs)
- Heavier than Phase 1. Maintain perfect hinge — no rounding.

### 6. Single-Arm Dumbbell Row `3 × 10 each side`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, rhomboids, rear delts, biceps
- **Clint**: 55–65 lb DB | **Wife**: 25–30 lb DB
- Heavier than Phase 1. Full stretch and full contraction.
- Superset with RDL. Rest 45–60 sec after completing both exercises each round.

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2: Vertical Pull + Rear Delt (cable machine)

### 7. Lat Pulldown (Wide Grip) `3 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, biceps, rear delts
- **Clint**: 120–135 lbs | **Wife**: 60–70 lbs
- Heavier than Phase 1. Control the return — no yanking.

### 8. Cable Face Pull `3 × 15`
- **Target muscles**: Rear delts, external rotators, rhomboids
- **Clint**: 50–60 lbs | **Wife**: 20–25 lbs
- Heavier than Phase 1. Same excellent form. Never rush face pulls.
- Superset with Lat Pulldown. Rest 45–60 sec after completing both exercises each round.

---

## Block 4 — Core Circuit (mat area)

### 9. Side Plank with Hip Dip `3 × 12 each side`
- More reps than Phase 1. Same control throughout.

### 10. Hollow Body Hold `3 × 30 sec`
- 10 sec longer than Phase 1. Lower back stays flat.

### 11. Cable Woodchop (Low to High) `3 × 12 each side`
- **Target muscles**: Obliques, core (anti-rotation), shoulder
- **Clint**: 40–50 lbs | **Wife**: 15–20 lbs
- Heavier than Phase 1. Drive through hips. Control the return.
- Minimal rest between core exercises.

---

## Block 5 — Finisher (5 min)

### 12. Sled Push or Kettlebell Swing `3 × 50 sec on / 10 sec off`
- Longer intervals than Phase 1. Maintain quality output.

---

## Notes
- Phase 2: prioritize load increase on RDL — it drives the most posterior chain development.
- Hollow body hold duration increases 10 sec per phase — core endurance builds progressively.
- Face pulls stay consistent in reps but increase in load — protect those shoulders.
