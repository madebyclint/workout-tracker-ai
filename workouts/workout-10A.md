# Week 10 — Workout A — Heavy Hip Thrust + Pull Complex + Core
**Phase**: 3 — Peak | **Week**: 10 of 12 | **Rounds**: 4 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Glutes · Back · Quads · Core (advanced stability)

> Rest 30–45 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Bike `3 min`

### 2. Clamshells `20 each side`

### 3. Glute Bridge March `15 each side`

### 4. Face Pull with Band `20 reps`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Glute Heavy + Row (bench/cable area) (4 rounds)

### 5. Barbell Hip Thrust `4 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Glutes, hamstrings, core
- **Clint**: bar + 45 + 35 lbs | **Wife**: bar + 25s
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 30–45 sec after each round.

### 6. Cable Row with Pause (mid-back squeeze) `4 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Mid-back, rhomboids, rear delts
- **Clint**: 140 lb | **Wife**: 65 lb

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Lunge + Fly (DB/cable area) (4 rounds)

### 7. Reverse Lunge with Knee Drive (weighted) `4 × 10 each leg`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, hip flexors
- **Clint**: 35 lb DBs | **Wife**: 18 lb DBs
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 30–45 sec after each round.

### 8. Cable Fly (mid height) `4 × 15`
- **Target muscles**: Chest, front delts
- **Clint**: 30 lb each side | **Wife**: 15 lb each side

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (4 rounds)

### 9. Ab Wheel Rollout from Feet (full) `4 × 6`

### 10. Copenhagen Plank `4 × 30 sec each side`

### 11. Pallof Press Squat to Press `4 × 10 each side`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Rowing Machine `4 × 1 min hard / 30 sec easy`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Hip thrust at this load is peak — record your weight.
- Ab wheel from feet is an advanced variation — back must stay neutral throughout.
