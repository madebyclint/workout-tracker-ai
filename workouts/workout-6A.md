# Week 6 — Workout A — Trap Bar Deadlift + Incline Press + Core
**Phase**: 2 — Build | **Week**: 6 of 12 | **Rounds**: 3 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Full Posterior Chain · Upper Chest · Core (flexion/rotation)

> Rest 45–60 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Elliptical `3 min`

### 2. Romanian Deadlift (bodyweight) `10 reps`

### 3. Thoracic Spine Rotation `10 each side`

### 4. Hip Flexor Stretch `30 sec each side`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Trap Bar Hinge + Push (barbell/bench area) (3 rounds)

### 5. Trap Bar Deadlift `3 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Full posterior chain, quads, core
- **Clint**: 135 lb | **Wife**: 65 lb
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 45–60 sec after each round.

### 6. Incline Dumbbell Press `3 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Upper chest, front delts, triceps
- **Clint**: 45 lb DBs | **Wife**: 22 lb DBs

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Lateral + Rear Chain (same area) (3 rounds)

### 7. Lateral Lunge to Curtsy Lunge `3 × 8 each side`
- **Target muscles**: Inner quads, glutes, adductors
- **Clint**: 20 lb DBs | **Wife**: 10 lb DBs
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 45–60 sec after each round.

### 8. Cable Face Pull with External Rotation `3 × 15`
- **Target muscles**: Rear delts, external rotators, rhomboids
- **Clint**: 35 lb | **Wife**: 18 lb

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (3 rounds)

### 9. Ab Wheel Rollout `3 × 10`

### 10. Side Plank with Cable Row `3 × 8 each side`

### 11. Lying Windshield Wipers (straight legs) `3 × 8 each side`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Stationary Bike `5 × 25 sec all-out / 35 sec easy`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Trap bar deadlift reduces lower back stress vs straight bar — allows heavier loading.
- Lateral-to-curtsy combo hits multiple planes — unique movement pattern.
