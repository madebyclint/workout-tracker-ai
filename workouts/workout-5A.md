# Week 5 — Workout A — Back Squat + Pull-Up Focus + Anti-Rotation Core
**Phase**: 2 — Build | **Week**: 5 of 12 | **Rounds**: 3 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Quads · Glutes · Lats · Hamstrings · Core (anti-rotation)

> Rest 45–60 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Treadmill (incline 4, 3.5 mph) `3 min`

### 2. Goblet Squat (bodyweight) `10 reps`

### 3. Scapular Pull-Up Hang `5 reps`

### 4. Hip Circles `10 each side`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Squat + Vertical Pull (rack/cable area) (3 rounds)

### 5. Barbell Back Squat (or Goblet) `3 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, core
- **Clint**: bar + 25s each side | **Wife**: 35 lb goblet
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 45–60 sec after each round.

### 6. Assisted Pull-Up `3 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, biceps, rear delts
- **Clint**: bodyweight | **Wife**: assisted (–40 lb)

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Hip Dominant + Horizontal Push (same zone) (3 rounds)

### 7. Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift (DB) `3 × 10 each leg`
- **Target muscles**: Hamstrings, glutes, balance
- **Clint**: 30 lb DBs | **Wife**: 15 lb DBs
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 45–60 sec after each round.

### 8. Cable Chest Press (bilateral, standing) `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Chest, front delts, core
- **Clint**: 45 lb each side | **Wife**: 22 lb each side

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (3 rounds)

### 9. Kneeling Pallof Press with Rotation `3 × 10 each side`

### 10. Hanging Knee Raise (slow) `3 × 12`

### 11. Plank with Alternating Leg Lift `3 × 10 each side`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Rowing Machine `4 × 45 sec hard / 15 sec rest`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Phase 2: add 5–10% load vs Phase 1. Rest 45–60 sec between supersets.
- Single-leg RDL is excellent for hiking and bouldering stability.
