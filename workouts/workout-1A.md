# Week 1 — Workout A — Lower Push + Vertical Pull + Core
**Phase**: 1 — Foundation | **Week**: 1 of 12 | **Rounds**: 3 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Quads · Glutes · Lats · Rear Delts · Core (stability)

> Rest 60–90 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Elliptical or Bike `3 min moderate pace`

### 2. Glute Bridge `15 reps`

### 3. Band Pull-Aparts `15 reps`

### 4. Leg Swings `10 each side`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Lower Body + Pull (rack/cable area) (3 rounds)

### 5. Goblet Squat `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, core
- **Clint**: 35–45 lb DB | **Wife**: 20–25 lb DB
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 60–90 sec after each round.

### 6. Lat Pulldown (Wide Grip) `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, biceps, rear delts
- **Clint**: 120 lb | **Wife**: 50–60 lb

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Glutes + Rear Delt (cable/bench area) (3 rounds)

### 7. Hip Thrust (Glute Bridge) `3 × 15`
- **Target muscles**: Glutes, hamstrings, core
- **Clint**: 45 lb DB on hips | **Wife**: 25 lb DB
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 60–90 sec after each round.

### 8. Cable Face Pull `3 × 15`
- **Target muscles**: Rear delts, external rotators, rhomboids
- **Clint**: 40 lb | **Wife**: 20 lb

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (3 rounds)

### 9. Dead Bug `3 × 10 each side`

### 10. Plank Hold `3 × 30 sec`

### 11. Side Plank `3 × 20 sec each side`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Rowing Machine `3 × 45 sec hard / 15 sec rest`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Goblet squat + lat pulldown: opposite patterns — ideal superset.
- Hip thrust: upper back on bench, drive heels, 2-sec glute squeeze at top.
- Face pulls are non-negotiable for long-term shoulder health.
