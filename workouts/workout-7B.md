# Week 7 — Workout B — Deficit RDL + Landmine Press + Core
**Phase**: 2 — Build | **Week**: 7 of 12 | **Rounds**: 3 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Hamstrings · Glutes · Shoulders · Core (rotation)

> Rest 45–60 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Rowing Machine `3 min`

### 2. Hamstring Stretch `45 sec each side`

### 3. Hip Circles `10 each side`

### 4. Scapular Push-Up `10 reps`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Elevated Hinge + Landmine Push (barbell corner) (3 rounds)

### 5. Deficit RDL (standing on plate) `3 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Hamstrings (increased range), glutes
- **Clint**: 55 lb DBs | **Wife**: 25 lb DBs
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 45–60 sec after each round.

### 6. Landmine Press (single arm) `3 × 10 each side`
- **Target muscles**: Shoulder, chest, core stability
- **Clint**: bar + 25 lb plate | **Wife**: bar only

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Cable Pull + Lunge (cable area) (3 rounds)

### 7. Cable Kickback (glute) `3 × 15 each leg`
- **Target muscles**: Glutes (peak contraction)
- **Clint**: 35 lb | **Wife**: 18 lb
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 45–60 sec after each round.

### 8. Reverse Lunge with Cable Row `3 × 10 each side`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, back, core
- **Clint**: 40 lb | **Wife**: 20 lb

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (3 rounds)

### 9. Hollow Body to Superman Roll `3 × 8 reps`

### 10. Copenhagen Plank `3 × 25 sec each side`

### 11. Cable Woodchop (Low to High, kneeling) `3 × 12 each side`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Jump Rope `4 × 1 min / 30 sec rest`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Deficit RDL increases hamstring stretch significantly — use lighter load initially.
- Reverse lunge with cable row: full-body challenge to balance and coordination.
