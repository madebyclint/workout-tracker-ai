# Week 6 — Workout B — Bulgarian Split Squat Heavy + Row + Core
**Phase**: 2 — Build | **Week**: 6 of 12 | **Rounds**: 3 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Quads · Glutes · Back · Core (stability/flexion)

> Rest 45–60 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Rowing Machine `3 min`

### 2. Hip Flexor Stretch `45 sec each side`

### 3. Lateral Band Walk `15 each side`

### 4. Band Glute Bridge `20 reps`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Single Leg Strength + Pull (bench/cable area) (3 rounds)

### 5. Bulgarian Split Squat (DB, heavy) `3 × 8 each leg`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, hip flexors
- **Clint**: 40 lb DBs | **Wife**: 20 lb DBs
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 45–60 sec after each round.

### 6. Seated Cable Row (close grip) `3 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Mid-back, lats, biceps
- **Clint**: 130 lb | **Wife**: 60 lb

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Glute + Chest (same zone) (3 rounds)

### 7. Cable Pull-Through (glute focus) `3 × 15`
- **Target muscles**: Glutes, hamstrings, lower back
- **Clint**: 60 lb | **Wife**: 30 lb
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 45–60 sec after each round.

### 8. Cable Crossover Fly `3 × 15`
- **Target muscles**: Chest, front delts
- **Clint**: 25 lb each side | **Wife**: 12 lb each side

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (3 rounds)

### 9. Dragon Flag Negative (slow lower) `3 × 5`

### 10. Pallof Press with Squat `3 × 10 each side`

### 11. Single-Leg Glute Bridge on Bench `3 × 12 each side`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Rowing Machine `3 × 1 min / 30 sec rest`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Bulgarian split squat Phase 2: increase load significantly.
- Dragon flag negative: lower slowly 3–5 sec. One of the most advanced core exercises.
