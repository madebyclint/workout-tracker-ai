# Workout A — Push + Core + Conditioning
**Phase**: 2 — Build | **Weeks**: 5–8 | **Est. Time**: 35–40 min
**Focus**: Chest · Shoulders · Quads · Glutes · Core (anti-extension)

> Add 5–10% load vs Phase 1 to all major lifts. Rest 45–60 sec between supersets.
> Rule: if you hit top of rep range with good form all 3 sets → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Cardio Warm-Up `2 min brisk walk or bike`
- Elevate heart rate. Loosen hips before loading.

### 2. World's Greatest Stretch `5 each side`
- Deep lunge + thoracic rotation.

### 3. Band Pull-Aparts `15 reps`
- Retract shoulder blades fully. Sets up scapular control.

### 4. Glute Bridge `15 reps`
- Bodyweight. Activate glutes before squatting.

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1: Lower Body (rack or floor area)

### 5. Goblet Squat `3 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, core
- **Clint**: 55–65 lb KB | **Wife**: 25–35 lb KB
- Heavier than Phase 1. Deeper squat. Superset with Hip Thrust.

### 6. Hip Thrust (Glute Bridge) `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Glutes, hamstrings, core
- **Clint**: 65–75 lb bar | **Wife**: 25–35 lbs (bar or DB)
- Weighted vs Phase 1. Controlled descent. Squeeze at top.
- Rest 45–60 sec after completing both exercises each round.

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2: Push Upper Body (cable or bench area)

### 7. Dumbbell Bench Press `3 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Chest, front delts, triceps
- **Clint**: 70–80 lbs total (2 DBs) | **Wife**: 25–35 lbs total
- Heavier than Phase 1. Still 3-sec eccentric. Chest focus.

### 8. Dumbbell Lateral Raise `3 × 15`
- **Target muscles**: Medial deltoids, shoulder health
- **Clint**: 18–22 lbs each | **Wife**: 6–10 lbs each
- Slight increase from Phase 1. Control remains priority.
- Rest 45–60 sec after completing both exercises each round.

---

## Block 4 — Core Circuit (mat or cable area)

### 9. Dead Bug `3 × 12 each side with 2-sec hold`
- Hold extended position 2 seconds per rep. Slow.

### 10. Pallof Press `3 × 12 each side`
- **Target muscles**: Core (anti-rotation), obliques
- **Clint**: 40–50 lbs | **Wife**: 15–20 lbs
- Heavier than Phase 1. Pause at full extension each rep.

### 11. Plank to Down Dog `3 × 12`
- Full range. Control each transition.
- Minimal rest between core exercises.

---

## Block 5 — Finisher (5 min)

### 12. Jump Rope or Rowing Machine `3 × 60 sec on / 15 sec off`
- Longer intervals than Phase 1. Maintain high intensity.

---

## Notes
- Phase 2 goal: 5–10% load increase on all major lifts from Phase 1.
- Core holds increased by 10 sec — hollow body and planks progress here.
- Longer finisher intervals = more metabolic stress = better fat loss stimulus.
- If load increase feels too heavy, stay at Phase 1 load one more session.
