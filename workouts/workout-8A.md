# Week 8 — Workout A — Power Complex + Core Intensive
**Phase**: 2 — Build | **Week**: 8 of 12 | **Rounds**: 3 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Posterior Chain · Lats · Quads · Core (all planes)

> Rest 45–60 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Elliptical `3 min`

### 2. Kettlebell Swing Practice (light) `10 reps`

### 3. Hip Hinge Drill `10 reps`

### 4. Band Clamshell `15 each side`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Power Hinge + Pull (kettlebell/cable area) (3 rounds)

### 5. Kettlebell Swing `3 × 15`
- **Target muscles**: Glutes, hamstrings, core (power)
- **Clint**: 35–44 lb KB | **Wife**: 18–26 lb KB
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 45–60 sec after each round.

### 6. Neutral Grip Pull-Up or Pulldown `3 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, biceps, rear delts
- **Clint**: bodyweight | **Wife**: assisted or 65 lb cable

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Quad + Press (bench/DB area) (3 rounds)

### 7. Leg Press (single leg) `3 × 10 each leg`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, hamstrings
- **Clint**: 110 lb | **Wife**: 55 lb
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 45–60 sec after each round.

### 8. Seated DB Press with Rotation `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Deltoids, triceps, rotator cuff
- **Clint**: 30 lb DBs | **Wife**: 12 lb DBs

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (3 rounds)

### 9. Hanging Leg Raise (straight legs) `3 × 10`

### 10. Pallof Press with Overhead Reach `3 × 10 each side`

### 11. Plank to Push-Up `3 × 8 each side`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Rowing Machine `4 × 45 sec hard / 15 sec rest`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- KB swing is best power-to-time exercise — drive hips explosively not arms.
- End of Phase 2: note all your loads for Phase 3 baseline.
