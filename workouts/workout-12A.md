# Week 12 — Workout A — Peak Full Body Complex + Core Showdown
**Phase**: 3 — Peak | **Week**: 12 of 12 | **Rounds**: 4 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Quads · Lats · Posterior Chain · Shoulders · Core (peak)

> Rest 30–45 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Rowing Machine (hard) `3 min`

### 2. Bodyweight Squat `15 reps`

### 3. Hip Hinge Drill `10 reps`

### 4. Arm Circles + Band Work `15 reps`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Squat + Pull Complex (rack/cable area) (4 rounds)

### 5. Barbell Back Squat `4 × 6`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, core
- **Clint**: bar + 45 + 10 lbs | **Wife**: 60 lb goblet
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 30–45 sec after each round.

### 6. Pull-Up (or Lat Pulldown) `4 × 6`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, biceps, rear delts
- **Clint**: bodyweight | **Wife**: assisted (–25 lb)

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Hinge + Press (barbell/bench area) (4 rounds)

### 7. Trap Bar or DB Deadlift `4 × 6`
- **Target muscles**: Full posterior chain, core
- **Clint**: 165 lb | **Wife**: 75 lb
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 30–45 sec after each round.

### 8. Standing DB Shoulder Press `4 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Deltoids, triceps, core
- **Clint**: 40 lb DBs | **Wife**: 18 lb DBs

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (4 rounds)

### 9. Hanging Leg Raise `3 × 12`

### 10. Side Plank `3 × 30 sec each side`

### 11. Cable Woodchop (Low to High) `3 × 12 each side`

### 12. Hollow Body Hold `3 × 30 sec`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 13. Stationary Bike `6 × 30 sec all-out / 30 sec rest`
- Max effort each interval.
