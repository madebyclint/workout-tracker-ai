# Week 8 — Workout B — Deadlift PR Attempt + Shoulder Superset + Core
**Phase**: 2 — Build | **Week**: 8 of 12 | **Rounds**: 3 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Posterior Chain · Shoulders · Core (flexion)

> Rest 45–60 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Treadmill Brisk Walk `3 min`

### 2. Hip Hinge with Band `10 reps`

### 3. Scapular Pull `10 reps`

### 4. Ankle Drill `10 each side`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Deadlift Heavy + Row (barbell area) (3 rounds)

### 5. Conventional Deadlift `3 × 6`
- **Target muscles**: Full posterior chain, core
- **Clint**: 155–175 lb | **Wife**: 75–85 lb
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 45–60 sec after each round.

### 6. Barbell or DB Bent-Over Row `3 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, rhomboids, rear delts, biceps
- **Clint**: 95 lb bar | **Wife**: 30 lb DBs

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Glute + Shoulder (same area) (3 rounds)

### 7. Sumo Squat with KB `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Inner quads, glutes, adductors
- **Clint**: 44 lb KB | **Wife**: 26 lb KB
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 45–60 sec after each round.

### 8. Cable Lateral Raise `3 × 15`
- **Target muscles**: Medial deltoids, shoulder health
- **Clint**: 15 lb each side | **Wife**: 8 lb each side

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (3 rounds)

### 9. Ab Wheel Rollout (feet elevated on bench) `3 × 8`

### 10. Side Bend (Cable, single arm) `3 × 15 each side`

### 11. Dead Bug with Band Pull `3 × 10 each side`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Stationary Bike `5 × 25 sec all-out / 35 sec easy`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Phase 2 deadlift: aim for a near-PR attempt. Record your weight.
- Cable lateral raise: keep shoulder health as a priority.
