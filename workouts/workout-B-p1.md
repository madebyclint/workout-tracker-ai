# Workout B — Pull + Hinge + Core + Conditioning
**Phase**: 1 — Foundation | **Weeks**: 1–4 | **Est. Time**: 35–40 min
**Focus**: Back · Hamstrings · Glutes · Rear Delts · Core (anti-lateral/rotation)

> Rest 60–90 sec between supersets. 90 sec between rounds. Learn the hip hinge — it's the foundation.
> Set up both stations in a circuit before starting the round.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Cardio Warm-Up `2 min row or elliptical`
- Moderate pace. Open up thoracic spine.

### 2. Cat-Cow `10 reps`
- Hands and knees. Full spinal flexion and extension. Slow.

### 3. Hip Hinge Drill `10 reps`
- Use wall or dowel. Groove the pattern before loading. Hinge at hips, not waist.

### 4. Face Pull with Band `15 reps`
- Pull band to forehead. External rotation at top. Warms up rear delts.

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1: Hinge + Pull (cable or free weight area)

### 5. Romanian Deadlift (RDL) `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Hamstrings, glutes, lower back
- **Clint**: 95–115 lbs (barbell) | **Wife**: 40–50 lbs (DBs)
- Soft knee bend. Bar/DBs drag down the legs. Feel hamstrings load at bottom.

### 6. Single-Arm Dumbbell Row `3 × 12 each side`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, rhomboids, rear delts, biceps
- **Clint**: 45–55 lb DB | **Wife**: 20–25 lb DB
- Support free hand on bench. Full stretch at bottom, full pull at top.
- Superset with RDL. Rest 60–90 sec after completing both exercises each round.

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2: Vertical Pull + Rear Delt (cable machine)

### 7. Lat Pulldown (Wide Grip) `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, biceps, rear delts
- **Clint**: 100–120 lbs | **Wife**: 50–60 lbs
- Wide grip. Pull to upper chest. Avoid close grip — reduces arm isolation.

### 8. Cable Face Pull `3 × 15`
- **Target muscles**: Rear delts, external rotators, rhomboids
- **Clint**: 40–50 lbs | **Wife**: 15–20 lbs
- Rope to forehead. External rotation at top. Critical for long-term shoulder health.
- Superset with Lat Pulldown. Rest 60 sec after completing both exercises each round.

---

## Block 4 — Core Circuit (mat area)

### 9. Side Plank with Hip Dip `3 × 10 each side`
- Hold side plank. Dip hip to floor and return. Anti-lateral core challenge.

### 10. Hollow Body Hold `3 × 20 sec`
- Arms overhead, legs low. Lower back pressed flat to floor the entire hold.

### 11. Cable Woodchop (Low to High) `3 × 10 each side`
- **Target muscles**: Obliques, core (anti-rotation), shoulder
- **Clint**: 30–40 lbs | **Wife**: 10–15 lbs
- Drive through hips and torso. Control the return.
- Minimal rest between core exercises.

---

## Block 5 — Finisher (5 min)

### 12. Sled Push or Kettlebell Swing `3 × 40 sec on / 20 sec off`
- All-out effort. Maximum power output each interval.

---

## Notes
- Romanian Deadlift is the foundation of posterior chain strength — prioritize form over load.
- Cable Face Pulls are non-negotiable for shoulder health — never skip them.
- Wide-grip lat pulldown reduces bicep emphasis vs close grip — back focus.
- Single-arm rows target lats; lat pulldowns reinforce vertical pull — different emphasis.
- Sled push is unmatched for metabolic conditioning. Row machine or KB swings are excellent substitutes.
