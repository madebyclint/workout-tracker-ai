# Week 2 — Workout B — Single Leg + Press + Rotational Core
**Phase**: 1 — Foundation | **Week**: 2 of 12 | **Rounds**: 3 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Quads · Glutes · Chest · Core (anti-rotation/rotation)

> Rest 60–90 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Rowing Machine `3 min easy`

### 2. Glute Bridge March `10 each side`

### 3. Thoracic Rotation `10 each side`

### 4. Ankle Circles `10 each side`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Single Leg + Press (free weight area) (3 rounds)

### 5. Bulgarian Split Squat (DB) `3 × 10 each leg`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, hip flexors
- **Clint**: 30 lb DBs | **Wife**: 15 lb DBs
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 60–90 sec after each round.

### 6. Cable Chest Press (single arm, standing) `3 × 12 each side`
- **Target muscles**: Chest, front delts, core stability
- **Clint**: 40 lb | **Wife**: 20 lb

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Glute + Rear Delt (cable area) (3 rounds)

### 7. Cable Kickback `3 × 15 each leg`
- **Target muscles**: Glutes (peak contraction)
- **Clint**: 30 lb | **Wife**: 15 lb
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 60–90 sec after each round.

### 8. Cable Rear Delt Fly `3 × 15`
- **Target muscles**: Rear delts, rhomboids
- **Clint**: 15 lb each side | **Wife**: 8 lb each side

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (3 rounds)

### 9. Cable Woodchop (Low to High) `3 × 12 each side`

### 10. Plank Shoulder Tap `3 × 12 each side`

### 11. Leg Raise (Flat Bench) `3 × 12`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Rowing Machine `3 × 1 min moderate / 30 sec rest`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Bulgarian split squat: most effective single-leg exercise — progress load steadily.
- Cable rear delt fly: full range, pause at peak contraction.
