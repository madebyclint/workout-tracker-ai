# Week 6 — Workout C — Active Recovery
**Phase**: 2 — Build | **Week**: 6 of 12 | **Est. Time**: 30 min
**Focus**: Mobility · Recovery · Light Activation · No Heavy Loading

> Low intensity — deliberate and continuous. This day accelerates recovery.
> Perform 2–3 slow rounds of the full flow if time allows.

---

## Block 1 — Foam Roll + Mobility

### 1. Foam Roll — Full Body `8 min`

### 2. 90/90 to Pigeon Flow `5 min`

### 3. Half-Kneeling Thoracic Rotation `10 each side`

---

## Block 2 — Stretching

### 4. Supine Hip Stretch `90 sec each side`

### 5. Thread the Needle `10 each side`

### 6. Side-Lying Quad Stretch `60 sec each`

---

## Block 3 — Light Activation

### 7. Band Walks (forward/backward/lateral) `20 each direction`

### 8. Slow Glute Bridge Breathing Drill `5 reps`

### 9. Prone T-Spine Extension `10 reps`

---

## Notes
- Foam roll slowly — pause 3–5 sec on tight spots.
- No grinding through pain. This is recovery, not training.
