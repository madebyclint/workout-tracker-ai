# Workout C — Active Recovery + Mobility
**Type**: Optional 3rd Day | **All Phases** | **Est. Time**: 30 min
**Focus**: Mobility · Flexibility · Light Activation · No Loading on Major Movers

> Low intensity — continuous flow. No heavy lifting. This day speeds up recovery.
> Perform as a slow, deliberate circuit x2–3 rounds.

---

## Block 1 — Foam Roll + Mobility

### 1. Foam Roll — Quads & IT Band `60 sec each side`
- Roll slowly. Pause on tight spots for 3–5 sec.

### 2. Foam Roll — Thoracic Spine `60 sec`
- Small extensions over the roller. Unlock mid-back.

### 3. 90/90 Hip Stretch `60 sec each side`
- Front and back leg at 90°. Sit tall. Deep hip opener.

---

## Block 2 — Flexibility + Movement

### 4. World's Greatest Stretch `5 each side`
- Lunge + thoracic rotation. Hold 2 sec each position. Full range.

### 5. Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch `60 sec each side`
- Front hip pushes slightly forward. Tall spine. Feel hip flexor lengthen.

### 6. Side-Lying Thoracic Rotation `10 each side`
- Knees stacked. Top arm sweeps to open chest. Follow hand with eyes.

---

## Block 3 — Light Core Activation

### 7. Band Pull-Aparts `20 reps`
- Light band. Retract shoulder blades. Posture reset.

### 8. Bear Crawl `20 yards forward + back`
- Hands and knees, knees hover 1 inch off floor. Slow and controlled.

### 9. Dead Bug (slow) `10 each side`
- No rush. Lower back flat. 3-second lowering each rep.

---

## Block 4 — Final Flow

### 10. Glute Bridge (bodyweight, slow) `15 reps`
- 2-sec hold at top. Drive through heels. Squeeze glutes.

### 11. Child's Pose to Cobra `10 reps`
- Full range. Breathe into each position. Close the session.

---

## Notes
- This day is not optional for progress — recovery is when adaptation happens.
- Wife: use this day to progress the hollow body and dead bug forms (she may find these easy).
- No load on quads, hamstrings, chest, or back — those muscles are recovering.
- Can be done at home if gym is not accessible.
