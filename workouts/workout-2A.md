# Week 2 — Workout A — Squat Pattern + Row + Core
**Phase**: 1 — Foundation | **Week**: 2 of 12 | **Rounds**: 3 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Quads · Back · Hamstrings · Core (flexion/rotation)

> Rest 60–90 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Bike `3 min`

### 2. Bodyweight Squat `15 reps`

### 3. Face Pull with Band `15 reps`

### 4. Hip Circles `10 each side`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Legs + Row (cable machine area) (3 rounds)

### 5. Leg Press `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, hamstrings
- **Clint**: 180–200 lb | **Wife**: 90–110 lb
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 60–90 sec after each round.

### 6. Seated Cable Row (Wide Grip) `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Mid-back, rhomboids, rear delts
- **Clint**: 120 lb | **Wife**: 55 lb

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Hip Dominant + Pull (same machine area) (3 rounds)

### 7. Lying Leg Curl `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Hamstrings, glutes
- **Clint**: 90 lb | **Wife**: 50 lb
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 60–90 sec after each round.

### 8. Cable Straight-Arm Pulldown `3 × 15`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, serratus, core
- **Clint**: 50 lb | **Wife**: 25 lb

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (3 rounds)

### 9. Ab Wheel Rollout (kneeling) `3 × 8`

### 10. Side Plank with Hip Lift `3 × 10 each side`

### 11. Lying Windshield Wipers (knees bent) `3 × 10 each side`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Jump Rope or Fast Feet `3 × 50 jumps / 30 sec rest`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Leg press: keep feet hip-width, full range of motion.
- Cable straight-arm pulldown: arms straight, lat-initiated — great isolation.
