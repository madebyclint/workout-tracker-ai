# Week 4 — Workout B — Sumo Deadlift + Press + Rotational Core
**Phase**: 1 — Foundation | **Week**: 4 of 12 | **Rounds**: 3 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Hamstrings · Inner Quads · Chest · Core (rotation)

> Rest 60–90 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Rowing Machine `3 min easy`

### 2. Sumo Squat (bodyweight) `10 reps`

### 3. Lateral Hip Stretch `10 each side`

### 4. Band Pull-Aparts `20 reps`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Wide Stance Hinge + Chest (barbell/bench area) (3 rounds)

### 5. Sumo Deadlift (DB or barbell) `3 × 10`
- **Target muscles**: Hamstrings, inner quads, glutes, adductors
- **Clint**: 95 lb bar | **Wife**: 30 lb DBs
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 60–90 sec after each round.

### 6. Dumbbell Floor Press `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Chest, triceps, front delts
- **Clint**: 45 lb DBs | **Wife**: 20 lb DBs

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Lunge Variation + Row (same area) (3 rounds)

### 7. Lateral Lunge (DB) `3 × 10 each side`
- **Target muscles**: Inner quads, glutes, adductors
- **Clint**: 25 lb DBs | **Wife**: 12 lb DBs
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 60–90 sec after each round.

### 8. Chest-Supported Incline Row (DB) `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Upper back, rhomboids, rear delts
- **Clint**: 35 lb DBs | **Wife**: 15 lb DBs

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (3 rounds)

### 9. Cable Woodchop (High to Low) `3 × 12 each side`

### 10. Hollow Body Rock `3 × 15 sec`

### 11. Ab Wheel Rollout `3 × 8`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Stationary Bike `5 × 20 sec sprint / 40 sec easy`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Sumo deadlift hits inner thighs and glutes differently from conventional — great variation.
- Chest-supported row removes lower back strain — excellent isolation.
