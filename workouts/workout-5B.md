# Week 5 — Workout B — Hip Thrust Loaded + Arnold Press + Core
**Phase**: 2 — Build | **Week**: 5 of 12 | **Rounds**: 3 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Glutes · Shoulders · Back · Core (lateral/flexion)

> Rest 45–60 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Bike `3 min`

### 2. Donkey Kick `15 each side`

### 3. Shoulder CARs `5 each side`

### 4. Glute Stretch `30 sec each side`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Glute + Shoulder (bench/DB area) (3 rounds)

### 5. Barbell Hip Thrust `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Glutes, hamstrings, core
- **Clint**: bar + 45 + 25 lbs | **Wife**: bar + 10s
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 45–60 sec after each round.

### 6. Arnold Press (seated) `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: All three deltoid heads, triceps
- **Clint**: 30 lb DBs | **Wife**: 12 lb DBs

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Row + Step (cable/bench area) (3 rounds)

### 7. Half-Kneeling Cable Row `3 × 12 each side`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, rhomboids, core stability
- **Clint**: 55 lb | **Wife**: 27 lb
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 45–60 sec after each round.

### 8. Weighted Step-Up (high bench) `3 × 10 each leg`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, hip flexors
- **Clint**: 30 lb DBs | **Wife**: 15 lb DBs

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (3 rounds)

### 9. Copenhagen Plank (inner thigh) `3 × 20 sec each side`

### 10. Cable Woodchop (High to Low) `3 × 12 each side`

### 11. Reverse Crunch with Hip Lift `3 × 15`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Jump Rope `5 × 40 sec / 20 sec rest`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Arnold press targets all three deltoid heads — superior to standard shoulder press.
- Half-kneeling row adds core anti-rotation demand.
