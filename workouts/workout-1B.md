# Week 1 — Workout B — Hinge + Horizontal Push + Core
**Phase**: 1 — Foundation | **Week**: 1 of 12 | **Rounds**: 3 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Hamstrings · Glutes · Chest · Shoulders · Core (anti-rotation)

> Rest 60–90 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Treadmill Brisk Walk `3 min (3.5–4 mph)`

### 2. Cat-Cow `10 reps`

### 3. Hip Hinge Drill (bodyweight) `10 reps`

### 4. World's Greatest Stretch `5 each side`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Hinge + Push (free weight area) (3 rounds)

### 5. Romanian Deadlift (DB) `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Hamstrings, glutes, lower back
- **Clint**: 50–60 lb DBs | **Wife**: 20–25 lb DBs
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 60–90 sec after each round.

### 6. Dumbbell Bench Press `3 × 12`
- **Target muscles**: Chest, front delts, triceps
- **Clint**: 45 lb DBs | **Wife**: 20 lb DBs

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Single Leg + Shoulder (same area) (3 rounds)

### 7. Reverse Lunge (DB) `3 × 10 each leg`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, hip flexors
- **Clint**: 30 lb DBs | **Wife**: 15 lb DBs
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 60–90 sec after each round.

### 8. Dumbbell Lateral Raise `3 × 15`
- **Target muscles**: Medial deltoids, shoulder health
- **Clint**: 15 lb each | **Wife**: 8 lb each

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (3 rounds)

### 9. Hollow Body Hold `3 × 20 sec`

### 10. Cable Pallof Press `3 × 12 each side`

### 11. Reverse Crunch `3 × 15`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Stationary Bike `4 × 30 sec sprint / 30 sec easy`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- RDL: soft knee bend, bar/DBs drag down the legs, feel hamstrings load at bottom.
- Lateral raises stay light intentionally — shoulder health and posture, not mass.
- No direct arm isolation across any session in this program.
