# Week 9 — Workout A — Heavy Squat + Chin-Up + Advanced Core
**Phase**: 3 — Peak | **Week**: 9 of 12 | **Rounds**: 4 | **Est. Time**: 40–45 min
**Focus**: Quads · Lats · Hamstrings · Upper Chest · Core (advanced)

> Rest 30–45 sec between supersets. Set up both stations before starting each round.
> Progression rule: complete all reps cleanly → add 5 lbs next session.

---

## Block 1 — Warm-Up (5 min)

### 1. Bike (resistance 8+) `3 min`

### 2. Glute Bridge `20 reps`

### 3. Deep Squat Hold `45 sec`

### 4. Band Pull-Aparts `20 reps`

---

## Block 2 — Circuit 1 — Squat Heavy + Pull (rack/cable area) (4 rounds)

### 5. Barbell Back Squat `4 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Quads, glutes, core
- **Clint**: bar + 35s each side | **Wife**: 50 lb goblet
- Superset 1A/1B: perform back-to-back. Rest 30–45 sec after each round.

### 6. Chin-Up (palms facing in) `4 × 6`
- **Target muscles**: Lats, biceps, rear delts
- **Clint**: bodyweight | **Wife**: assisted (–30 lb)

---

## Block 3 — Circuit 2 — Posterior + Push (bench/DB area) (4 rounds)

### 7. Romanian Deadlift (heavy) `4 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Hamstrings, glutes, lower back
- **Clint**: 70 lb DBs | **Wife**: 30 lb DBs
- Superset 2A/2B: perform back-to-back. Rest 30–45 sec after each round.

### 8. Incline Dumbbell Press `4 × 8`
- **Target muscles**: Upper chest, front delts, triceps
- **Clint**: 50 lb DBs | **Wife**: 25 lb DBs

---

## Block 4 — Core Block (4 rounds)

### 9. Dragon Flag (controlled) `4 × 5`

### 10. Kneeling Cable Crunch `4 × 15`

### 11. Side Plank with Cable Pull `4 × 8 each side`

---

## Block 5 — Finisher

### 12. Rowing Machine `5 × 45 sec hard / 15 sec rest`
- Max effort each interval.

---

## Notes
- Phase 3: heaviest loads, 4 rounds, 30–45 sec rest. Maximum effort.
- Dragon flag: full-body tension required — one of the most advanced core exercises.
